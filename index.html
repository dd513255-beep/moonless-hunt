Moonless Hunt Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026 · Raemi Studio

1. Information we collect

Moonless Hunt (the "Game") does not collect any personal information. It does not ask for, store or send your name, email, location, device identifiers, contacts, photos or usage data.

2. Data stored on your device

Your progress (towns cleared, coins, unlocked cards and settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to Raemi Studio or anyone else, and it is removed when you delete the Game.

3. Network, ads and payments

  • The Game does not connect to the internet.
  • There are no ads and no in-app purchases.
  • The Game uses no analytics, crash-reporting or ad-tracking tools.

4. Third parties

Because we collect nothing, we share nothing. Purchases and downloads happen through the Apple App Store and are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Children

The Game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new text and effective date will be posted on this page. If a future update ever collects data, this policy will be updated before that update is released.
